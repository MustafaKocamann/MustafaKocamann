### About me

I'm an **AI Engineer and Data Scientist** working at the intersection of **LLM applications, agentic systems, machine learning, and data-driven products**.

My focus is not just on getting a model to produce an answer, but on designing the system around it: **context and retrieval, tool orchestration, evaluation, safety, deployment, and observability**. I approach engineering from both a technical and product perspective, connecting real-world problems to measurable outcomes.

### What I focus on

- **Agentic AI & LLM applications** — Stateful workflows, tool-using agents, memory, and reliable orchestration.
- **RAG & knowledge systems** — Retrieval pipelines, vector search, graph-based knowledge, and context quality.
- **LLM evaluation & AI reliability** — Offline/online evaluation, tracing, guardrails, and operational visibility.
- **Machine learning & data science** — Predictive modeling, experimentation, and statistical analysis.
- **Time-series analysis** — Working with temporal data, forecasting problems, and time-series projects.
- **End-to-end delivery** — APIs, data integration, containerization, cloud infrastructure, and monitoring.

### How I approach engineering

```text
Business problem
    ↓
Data, constraints & success metrics
    ↓
Architecture & integration
    ↓
Implementation & evaluation
    ↓
Security, deployment & monitoring
    ↓
Iteration based on real-world behavior
```

I care about the trade-offs that determine whether an AI system is useful in practice:

- **Reliability:** measurable quality, regression testing, and failure-aware workflows.
- **Efficiency:** latency, inference cost, retrieval quality, and appropriate model selection.
- **Safety:** controlled tool access, validation, and guardrails for sensitive operations.
- **Operations:** actionable telemetry, traceability, deployment reproducibility, and maintainability.
- **Product impact:** aligning technical decisions with user needs, adoption, and business value.
